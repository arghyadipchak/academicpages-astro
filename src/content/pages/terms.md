## Privacy Policy

The privacy of visitors to this personal academic website is respected. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that may be collected and how it is used.

### Direct Inquiries

If you contact me via email or through links provided on this site, your email address and any submitted information are used solely to reply to your inquiry. Your contact information is never sold, shared, or distributed.

### Hosting & Infrastructure

This website is statically generated and hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosting platforms, standard server access logs may be recorded by the hosting provider for security and operational purposes:

- Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
- Browser types and operating systems
- Date and time stamps
- Referring and exit pages

These logs are maintained by the infrastructure provider and are not combined with personally identifiable information.

### Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) solely to remember your preferred visual appearance theme (light or dark mode). No tracking cookies, marketing scripts, or third-party advertising services are used on this site.
